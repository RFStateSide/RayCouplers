# Licensing preparation and review boundary

Copyright holder: RF StateSide LLC, as owner confirmed. Hardware direction: CERN-OHL-P v2.0; SPDX CERN-OHL-P-2.0. The root [LICENSE](../LICENSE) is the unmodified official permissive text obtained from the [CERN/OHWR source repository](https://gitlab.com/ohwr/project/cernohl/-/raw/master/licence_texts/cern_ohl_p_v2.txt). Acquisition URL, size and SHA-256 are recorded in license-provenance.json. The license text is copyright CERN 2020; the project copyright notice is kept separately in [NOTICE](../NOTICE).

The official license addresses retaining notices when conveying source (3.1–3.3), recording dated modifications (3.3), supplying the license when conveying under different terms (3.4), and access to applicable notices for product recipients (4). Its warranty/liability provisions are in section 5. This is a review aid, not replacement legal language. No terms were altered. Read the official text for the actual conditions.

Notices are prepared in README, NOTICE, KiCad title-block comments, BOM documentation, manufacturing fabrication notes and the manufacturing ZIP. On-board historic artwork is retained; no extra copper or silkscreen was added for a legal notice. The prepared distribution notice applies to RF StateSide-owned curated hardware source and related engineering documentation. It does not claim ownership of vendor CAD assets, KiCad library collections or interactive BOM software.

Library collections require separate provenance/attribution review, described in [asset provenance](asset-provenance.md). Original private archives are preserved and excluded from the proposed initial repository. Vendor datasheets/models are linked rather than bundled. Original Excel creator metadata and personal/order records require owner selection before inclusion.

Engineering-reference statements describe AS-IS status, lack of guaranteed performance/fitness/certification and builder responsibility for evaluation. They do not amend the CERN license and do not establish that RF StateSide LLC or its personnel are free from every possible liability. Final license suitability, company attribution, third-party obligations and liability wording require owner and appropriate legal review before public publication. This phase performed preparation only.
